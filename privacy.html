Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StaffSaaS ("StaffSaaS", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the StaffSaaS website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Important: StaffSaaS is a workforce-management platform used by businesses and their employees. A business that provides you with access to StaffSaaS may control the employee and workplace information entered into its account. If you use StaffSaaS through your employer, you should also contact your employer about its privacy practices and requests concerning workplace data.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how a business configures and uses the Services, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: information used to create and manage an account, such as your name, email address, username, and account or employee identifier.
  • Business and employment information: business or branch association, employee role, employment-related identifiers, and other information entered by an authorised business administrator.
  • Scheduling information: rosters, shifts, work dates, shift times, and shift-related details.
  • Availability and leave information: availability preferences, submitted leave requests, requested dates, request status, and related details entered by users or business administrators.
  • Device and notification information: device identifiers or push-notification tokens used to deliver notifications, where you enable or use notification functionality.
  • Support and communications: information you provide when contacting us for assistance or reporting a problem.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated when using the Services, such as application version, device type, operating-system version, and diagnostic or error information, where collected by the app or its service providers.

Please do not submit information that is not required for the intended workplace function. Businesses are responsible for ensuring that information they enter into StaffSaaS is appropriate and that they have a lawful basis to provide it.

2. How We Use Information

We use information, as applicable, to:

  • create, authenticate, and manage user accounts;
  • provide staff scheduling, roster, shift, availability, and leave-management features;
  • associate employees with the correct business and branch;
  • send service-related notifications, including roster or shift updates and other relevant alerts;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services;
  • respond to support requests and communicate important service information;
  • meet applicable legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Services.

3. How Information Is Shared

We may share or make information available in the following circumstances:

  • With your business: information associated with a business account may be accessible to that business's authorised administrators and relevant personnel so they can manage workforce operations.
  • With service providers: trusted providers may process information on our behalf to host, operate, maintain, secure, or support the Services. They may only use information as permitted for the services they provide and subject to applicable obligations.
  • For legal and safety reasons: where required by law, legal process, or a valid request, or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of some or all of our business, subject to appropriate protections.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes through this Privacy Policy.

4. Push Notifications

StaffSaaS may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a service provided by Google, to deliver push notifications to supported devices. To enable delivery, the app may use a device push-notification token and relevant notification data. Notifications may relate to shifts, roster changes, leave requests, or other workforce activity.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings. Disabling notifications may prevent you from receiving alerts, but does not necessarily prevent you from accessing other app features.

5. Data Storage and Security

StaffSaaS uses a backend service and database to operate account and workforce functionality. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security. Access to information should be limited to people and service providers who need it for legitimate operational purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, support the relevant business account, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or contractual obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the instructions or lawful requirements applicable to the business account.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal, operational, and backup requirements.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

If you use StaffSaaS through an employer or another business, contact that business first for requests relating to employee records, rosters, shifts, availability, or leave information. You may also contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the relevant business before responding.

You can manage push-notification permissions through your device settings. You should not share your password with other people and should notify the relevant business or us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorisation.

8. Children's Privacy

StaffSaaS is a workforce-management service intended for use by businesses and their personnel. It is not designed to be directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to StaffSaaS, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

9. International Use and Data Transfers

StaffSaaS may be accessed by businesses and employees in different regions. Information may be processed or stored in the locations where StaffSaaS or its service providers operate. Where information is transferred across borders, we take steps required by applicable privacy laws to protect it.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

The Services may rely on third-party infrastructure or services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy information published by those providers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Services, our practices, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
